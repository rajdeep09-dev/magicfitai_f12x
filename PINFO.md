# F12X × MAGICFIT Platform Architecture

This document defines the business logic and system architecture for the influencer marketing platform.

---

## 1. The Creator Lifecycle
*   **Entry:** Creators are currently manually sourced by Editors. We will maintain an "Outreach Pipeline" Kanban board where Editors track prospects before adding them to the database.
*   **Creator Portal:** Currently, creators **do not** have a login. The Editor acts as the "Content Gateway," handling all video uploads and payment link configurations to ensure high quality and brand compliance.

## 2. The Financial & Logic Engine
*   **Pending Payouts:** Upon "Client Approval," the system automatically creates a record in the `payouts` table with a status of `pending`.
*   **Tolt Integration:** Tolt is the primary rail for **tracking** affiliate sales. Flat fees for creators are managed via the platform's `payouts` logic.
*   **Budget Refunds:** If a client changes their mind after approval, the Editor must manually "reject" the payout, which triggers a `setBudget` function to release the funds back into the campaign pool automatically.

## 3. Content & Approval Pipeline
*   **Revisions:** Supports unlimited "Revision Requests" until the final asset is approved. The PDF Export workflow serves as the formal revision document.
*   **Analytics Trigger:** Performance data (Views/Likes) is updated manually by the Editor or synced via a scheduled batch task 24 hours after the `live_date` to ensure stable numbers.

## 4. The Client Experience
*   **Visibility:** Clients see the "Total Invoice" (Base + Commission + Tax). They do **not** see the breakdown of F12X agency costs.
*   **Communication:** Communication happens outside the app (via Email links generated by the PDF Export workflow) to keep the portal UI clean and lightweight.

## 5. Scaling & Multi-Tenancy
*   **Model:** Currently supporting Single Roster (One Brand Campaign).
*   **Future-Proofing:** The schema uses `campaign_id` across all tables, allowing us to pivot to multi-campaign support in the future by simply adding a "Select Campaign" dropdown in the Header.
