PHASE 1: PREPARE GOOGLE SHEET (3 minutes)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

□ Go to Google Sheets: https://sheets.google.com
□ Create new spreadsheet
□ Name it: "Magicfit Campaign Creators" (or your choice)
□ In Row 1, add these headers:
  id | campaign_id | creator_name | platform | deliverable | approval_status | progress_score | live_date | video_link | published_video_link | views | engagement_rate | spend
□ In Row 2+, add your creator data
□ Make sure sheet is named "Sheet1" (the default)
□ Save the spreadsheet

PHASE 2: SHARE WITH SERVICE ACCOUNT (2 minutes)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

□ Click "Share" button (top right of Google Sheet)
□ Paste this email: farooqxf12x@farooq-dashboard.iam.gserviceaccount.com
□ Select "Viewer" permission (read-only)
□ Click "Share"

PHASE 3: GET SPREADSHEET ID (1 minute)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

□ Copy the URL: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/[ID]/edit

PHASE 5: WAIT FOR REDEPLOY (2 minutes)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

□ Vercel auto-redeploys after adding environment variables
□ Go to Vercel Deployments tab to check progress
□ Wait for green "Ready" status (about 1-2 minutes)
□ Check that all 12 variables show as "Added"

PHASE 6: TEST THE INTEGRATION (2 minutes)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

□ Go to your portal URL
□ Open browser console (F12 or right-click → Inspect → Console)
□ Refresh the page
□ Look for one of these messages:

   SUCCESS: [v0] Successfully loaded X creators from Google Sheets
   
   OR (still works, but using fallback)
   
   FALLBACK: [v0] Using fallback mock data (Google Sheets not configured)

□ If you see the SUCCESS message, you're done! ✅
□ If you see FALLBACK, check troubleshooting below

TROUBLESHOOTING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Problem: Still seeing "Using fallback mock data"
Solution:
  □ Check all 12 environment variables are set in Vercel
  □ Verify GOOGLE_SHEETS_SPREADSHEET_ID matches your sheet exactly
  □ Go to your Google Sheet → Click Share → Verify email is listed
  □ Wait 2 minutes, then refresh browser
  □ Check Vercel Deployments - is it fully deployed? (green "Ready")

Problem: "Permission denied" error in console
Solution:
  □ Open your Google Sheet
  □ Click Share button
  □ Verify farooqxf12x@farooq-dashboard.iam.gserviceaccount.com is listed
  □ Make sure it has "Viewer" permission (not just "Can comment")
  □ Click Share again to confirm

Problem: App loads but shows wrong data
Solution:
  □ Check Google Sheet column headers are exactly:
    id | campaign_id | creator_name | platform | deliverable | approval_status | progress_score | live_date | video_link | published_video_link | views | engagement_rate | spend
  □ Check Platform values are: Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube
  □ Check Status values are: Ideation, Script Sent, Video Pending Approval, Revisions Requested, Approved, or Published
  □ Check dates are in format: YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2026-05-15)
